About ODEX Drilling

ODEX drilling combines rotary and percussion techniques to efficiently penetrate overburden and unstable formations.

Safe Overburden Penetration

Ideal for construction, groundwater, and mining preparatory drilling where ground stability is a concern.

6½" Borewell Drilling Depth Details

Our 6½" borewell drilling depth capability is engineered for deep water extraction in challenging ground conditions. This size is ideal for projects that require higher discharge rates and more stable bore configurations.

  • Typical drilling depths: up to 280 meters (depending on geology)
  • Recommended for deep aquifers and industrial water supply
  • Optimized for high-capacity recovery and long-term well performance
