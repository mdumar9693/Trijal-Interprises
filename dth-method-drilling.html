DTH Method Drilling

Advanced down-the-hole drilling services for deep and hard rock applications.

Why Choose DTH Method?

DTH drilling delivers powerful percussion drilling through hard rock with speed, accuracy, and reduced casing requirements.

Deep Rock Penetration

DTH drilling is ideal for deep borewells, mining shafts, and foundation drilling where rock conditions are severe.

High impact, consistent advance rates, and precise bore control.

Key Advantages

  • Fast drilling in hard rock
  • Minimal deviation and precise bore alignment
  • Robust performance in deep and layered formations

Related Borewell Drilling Sizes

4½" Borewell Drilling

Our 4½" borewell drilling is a cost-effective solution for residential, agricultural, and small commercial water supply needs. It delivers reliable water yield with efficient drilling performance in moderate ground conditions.

6½" Borewell Drilling

The 6½" borewell drilling service is designed for deep, high-capacity water extraction in demanding geological formations. This size offers enhanced stability and greater discharge potential, making it ideal for industrial and large agricultural projects.
