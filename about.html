About Us

TRIJAL ENTERPRISES is a premier drilling contractor with over 15 years of excellence in the industry. Founded in 2009, we have established ourselves as a leading provider of comprehensive drilling solutions across Jharkhand, Bihar, and West Bengal.

Our journey began with a simple vision: to deliver world-class drilling services with unwavering commitment to quality, safety, and customer satisfaction. Today, we are proud to serve hundreds of satisfied clients across residential, commercial, industrial, and mining sectors.

With a fleet of modern equipment, a team of highly skilled professionals, and a commitment to continuous innovation, we have become the trusted partner for drilling projects of all scales and complexities.
