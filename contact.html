Frequently Asked Questions

How can I get a quote for my project?

You can get a free quote by filling out the contact form above, calling us directly, or sending an email with your project details. Our team will contact you within 24 hours to discuss your requirements.

What is your response time?

We aim to respond to all inquiries within 24 hours during business days. For urgent matters, you can call us directly or reach out via WhatsApp for immediate assistance.

Do you provide equipment rental services?

Yes, we offer comprehensive equipment rental services with flexible terms. We provide daily, weekly, monthly, or long-term rental options with free delivery and setup.

Do you operate in other states besides Jharkhand, Bihar, and West Bengal?

Currently, we primarily operate in Jharkhand, Bihar, and West Bengal. However, we can discuss special arrangements for projects in nearby regions. Please contact us for more information.

What safety certifications do you have?

We are ISO 9001:2015, ISO 45001:2018, and ISO 14001:2015 certified. We maintain the highest standards of quality, occupational health & safety, and environmental management.

Can you handle emergency or urgent drilling projects?

Yes, we can handle emergency and urgent projects. Our team is available 24/7 for emergency situations. Please contact us immediately via phone or WhatsApp for urgent assistance.
