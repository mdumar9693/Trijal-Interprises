Specialized Solutions

Drilling Methods & Additional Services

We provide advanced drilling techniques and comprehensive drilling solutions tailored to industrial, mining, and borewell requirements.

Precision drilling with proven methods

From DTH and ODEX to rotary drilling, our team brings the right technology and expertise to every site for safe, efficient, and reliable results.

DTH Method Drilling

Down-The-Hole drilling method designed for fast and efficient deep drilling operations.

Ideal for hard rock penetration and high-volume drilling projects.

ODEX Method Drilling

Advanced overburden drilling technique suitable for unstable ground and difficult formations.

Delivers controlled drilling in soft strata and mixed ground conditions.

Rotary Method Drilling

Reliable rotary drilling solutions for borewells, mining, and geological applications.

A cost-effective choice for sedimentary and alluvial formations.
